# App Privacy Information - App Store Connect

## Privacy Policy

This document outlines the data collection and usage practices for the نشأة iOS application, as required by Apple's App Store Connect privacy questionnaire.

---

## Data Collection Summary

### Does this app collect data? **Yes**

---

## Data Types Collected

### 1. Contact Info

| Data Type | Collected | Linked to Identity | Used for Tracking |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Name | Yes | Yes | No |
| Email Address | Yes | Yes | No |
| Phone Number | Yes | Yes | No |

**Purpose:** App Functionality, Account Management
**Explanation:** Parent and staff names, emails, and phone numbers are collected during account creation by the nursery administrator for communication and identification purposes.

---

### 2. Health & Fitness

| Data Type | Collected | Linked to Identity | Used for Tracking |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Health (allergies, medical conditions) | Yes | Yes | No |

**Purpose:** App Functionality
**Explanation:** Children's medical information (allergies, conditions, medications) is stored to ensure their safety while at the nursery. This data is entered by parents and visible only to authorized staff.

---

### 3. Photos or Videos

| Data Type | Collected | Linked to Identity | Used for Tracking |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Photos | Yes | Yes | No |

**Purpose:** App Functionality
**Explanation:** Child profile photos are uploaded for identification. Message attachments may include photos shared between parents and teachers regarding child activities.

---

### 4. Identifiers

| Data Type | Collected | Linked to Identity | Used for Tracking |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| User ID | Yes | Yes | No |
| Device ID (Push Token) | Yes | Yes | No |

**Purpose:** App Functionality, Push Notifications
**Explanation:** User IDs are used for authentication. Device push tokens are stored to deliver real-time notifications for pickup alerts, messages, and daily reports.

---

### 5. Usage Data

| Data Type | Collected | Linked to Identity | Used for Tracking |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Product Interaction | Yes | Yes | No |

**Purpose:** App Functionality
**Explanation:** Attendance records, pickup timestamps, and message read receipts are tracked as core app functionality (not for analytics or advertising).

---

### 6. Sensitive Data

| Data Type | Collected | Linked to Identity | Used for Tracking |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Biometric Data | No | No | No |

**Purpose:** N/A
**Explanation:** Face ID / Touch ID is used for local device authentication only. Biometric data never leaves the device and is not collected by the app or server.

---

## Data NOT Collected

The following data types are **NOT** collected by this app:

- Financial Info (payment card numbers, bank accounts)
- Location (precise or coarse)
- Browsing History
- Search History
- Contacts (address book)
- Diagnostics (crash data, performance data)
- Advertising Data
- Purchases

---

## Data Linked to You

All collected data is linked to the user's identity through their authenticated account.

## Data Used to Track You

**No data is used to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.**

---

## Third-Party Data Sharing

| Third Party | Data Shared | Purpose |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| None | None | N/A |

This app does **not** share any user data with third parties for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

---

## Data Retention

- Account data is retained while the user's account is active
- Attendance and report history is retained for the academic year
- Messages are retained until archived or deleted by admin
- Users can request data deletion through the nursery administration

---

## Security Measures

- All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3
- Authentication via secure OAuth 2.0 tokens
- Biometric authentication for device-level security
- Role-based access control (parents see only their children's data)
- Server-side data encrypted at rest
- Push notification tokens stored securely

---

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This app handles data related to children under 13. However:
- The app is used by **parents and staff** (adults), not by children directly
- Children's data is entered and managed by authorized adults only
- No child can create an account or interact with the app
- The app complies with COPPA requirements for handling children's information

---

## App Tracking Transparency

This app does **NOT** use the AppTrackingTransparency framework because:
- No advertising SDKs are included
- No cross-app tracking occurs
- No data is shared with data brokers
- No advertising identifiers (IDFA) are collected

---

## Required Privacy Nutrition Labels (App Store Connect)

When filling out the App Store Connect privacy questionnaire, select:

1. **Contact Info** → Name, Email, Phone → App Functionality → Linked to Identity
2. **Health & Fitness** → Health → App Functionality → Linked to Identity
3. **Photos or Videos** → Photos → App Functionality → Linked to Identity
4. **Identifiers** → User ID, Device ID → App Functionality → Linked to Identity
5. **Usage Data** → Product Interaction → App Functionality → Linked to Identity

For all items:
- "Used to Track You" = **No**
- "Linked to Identity" = **Yes**
